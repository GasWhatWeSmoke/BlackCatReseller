# OCR engine: PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 (a fallback; QR is the primary decode path).
# OCR is deliberately CPU-only. decode.py pins every compatible PaddleOCR
# constructor to device="cpu" so scan/decode does not consume local-model GPU VRAM
# admission without contending for its GPU lane. Moving OCR to GPU requires a
# separately reviewed admission/order change, not a dependency swap. It also
# passes enable_mkldnn=False to avoid a OneDNN PIR crash in Paddle 3.x PP-OCRv5.
